
# Sony Professional Europe
## SEO & AI Search Visibility Dashboard – Briefing Document

**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** Baresquare  
**Stakeholder:** Sony Professional Europe (PSE) – Marketing, Digital, SEO & Strategy teams

---

## 1. Background & Objective

Sony Professional Europe is expanding its SEO and AI Search monitoring capabilities.  
The objective of this dashboard is to provide:

- **Comprehensive SEO visibility insights** powered by Google Search Console  
- **Accurate organic traffic reporting** from Adobe Analytics  
- **Brand and content presence tracking in AI-driven search tools**  
- **Topic and content performance monitoring** across key areas such as Broadcast, Production, IP Live, Cloud, and Professional Solutions  
- **Knowledge Content (Glossary/Lexicon-style) performance signals** integrated seamlessly in SEO and AI tabs  

Region focus:  
**Europe (PSE)** is the *default scope* for all visualizations unless a breakdown view is explicitly provided.

---

## 2. Tools & Data Sources

### **Google Search Console**
- Ranking keywords  
- Impressions, clicks, CTR  
- SERP features (PAA, Featured Snippets, Videos, Images)  
- Keyword buckets (Top 3, 4–10, 11–20, 21–50, 51–100)  
- Country segmentation for Europe  

### **Adobe Analytics**
- Organic traffic sessions  
- Engagement signals (bounce, time on page, scroll if available)  
- AI/LLM referrer traffic  

### **AI / LLM Traffic Sources**
- ChatGPT  
- Perplexity  
- Bing Copilot  
- Google AI Overviews  
- Anthropic Claude  
- Mistral  
- Gemini  
- Other AI-powered browsing engines  

---

## 3. Dashboard Tabs

The dashboard contains **three main tabs** plus **one integrated section** for Knowledge Content Performance.

---

# TAB 1 — Performance Overview

A high-level executive snapshot that combines the essential SEO and organic traffic insights.

### **Components**
#### **1.1 Organic Visibility Summary (GSC)**
- Total ranking keywords  
- Trend line for impressions & clicks  
- Ranking bucket summary (Top 3, Top 10, etc.)  
- SERP feature count (PAA, FS, Video, Image)

#### **1.2 Organic Traffic Overview (Adobe Analytics)**
- Organic sessions trend  
- Top landing pages  
- Traffic change MoM / YoY  

#### **1.3 Monthly Insights (Auto-generated)**
- Highlights wins, issues, emerging opportunities  

---

# TAB 2 — Keyword, Content & Market Performance

Combines keyword insights, content effectiveness, competitor tracking, and regional segmentation.

### **Components**

#### **2.1 Keyword Performance (GSC)**
- Keyword bucket trend chart (Top 3, 4–10, etc.)  
- New keyword entries  
- Keywords gaining positions  
- Keywords losing visibility  

#### **2.2 Content Performance (GSC + Adobe)**
- Top performing pages  
- Underperforming pages  
- Suggested optimizations  
- **Knowledge Content Performance** (Glossary/Lexicon-style content)

#### **2.3 Competitor Market Overview**
Competitors:
- Grass Valley  
- EVS  
- Ross Video  
- Vizrt  
- Imagine Communications  
- Blackmagic  

Metrics:
- Share of Voice  
- Ranking overlap  
- Competitor SERP features  

#### **2.4 Europe Country Breakdown**
- Organic traffic by EU country  
- Ranking visibility by country  
- Top EU markets contributing to organic performance

- Perplexity
